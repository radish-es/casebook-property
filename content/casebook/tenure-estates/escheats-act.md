2 The Governor in Council may direct the Attorney General to take proceedings to revest in the Crown

(a) any lands comprised in any grant, or any part thereof, which have become forfeited to the Crown for non-fulfilment of the condition in the grant;

(b) any or all lands of persons dying intestate and leaving no heirs or persons entitled to the said lands under the laws of the Province.
