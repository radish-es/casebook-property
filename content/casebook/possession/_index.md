Possession as a founding concept in Anglo-Canadian law.

## First Possession

Anglo-Canadian common law has often afforded possession--as physical dominion over a thing combined with an intention to control it--a significant place in questions about the origins of property, the allocation and distribution of resources, and the prioritization of different property relations.
